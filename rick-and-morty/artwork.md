# Rick and Morty artwork

The 9 wallpapers are art inspired by Rick and Morty, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Adult Swim, Williams Street and their creators Justin Roiland and Dan Harmon. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-through-the-portal.jpg`: Rick and Morty through the portal; artist unknown
- `02-dimensional-drifts.jpg`: Dimensional drifts; artist unknown
- `03-portal-swirl.jpg`: Rick and Morty in the portal swirl; artist unknown
- `04-mad-scientist.jpg`: Rick Sanchez, mad scientist; artist unknown
- `05-mastermind-of-mayhem.jpg`: Rick Sanchez, mastermind of mayhem; artist unknown
- `06-rick-in-the-dark.jpg`: Rick in the dark; artist unknown
- `07-red-moon.jpg`: Rick and Morty under the red moon; artist unknown
- `08-breaking-bad.jpg`: Rick and Morty in the desert; artist unknown
- `09-existence-is-pain.jpg`: Mr. Meeseeks: Existence is pain; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: dark space background, lab-coat white text, portal green accent, red moon, portal green, Morty yellow, Rick blue, cosmic violet and Meeseeks teal.
